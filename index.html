Welcome to Ukwuata Global Initiatives! 🎓

Talent is equally distributed, but opportunity is not.

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in South Bend, Indiana, with a mission to bridge the gap between exceptional graduates and global PhD opportunities.

Where does our name come from?

In Igbo, "Ukwuata" speaks to growth and abundance. Additionally, one of our co-founders' native village in Nigeria was founded by a man named Ukwuata. We use his name to honor his legacy of hardworking, perseverant men and woman who are his children.

We believe that when we invest in one scholar, we invest in a community, a nation, and the world. We are officially incorporated and working towards making quality mentorship, career services and financial assistance accessible to those who need it most.

Follow us to be part of this journey. If you are interested in supporting this initiative, send us a message via Facebook. The future is global, collaborative and will be better when more people have access to professional opportunities.
